About MedFinder

MedFinder helps someone on a long-term US prescription check whether the exact same medicine is sold in India, and what a 90-day supply would cost delivered.

A match counts only when the ingredient, salt, strength, form, and release type all agree. Those rules are code in match.js, not the chat model. The chat model only collects details. It never decides a match, a price, or a seller, and it doesn't give medical advice.

Saved medicines and refill reminders stay in this browser. MedFinder doesn't take payment or ship medicine.
