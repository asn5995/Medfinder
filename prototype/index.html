Prescription

Upload a photo or PDF. The reader fills the medicine, strength, frequency, and quantity when it can see them. Check or edit the fields, then save. An unreadable file or a file that is not a prescription stays unsaved.

Orders and refills

Maintenance medicines are treated as ongoing. The next refill is the supply length after the order date. The default supply is 60 days, and the default reminder is 20 days before that date. An order here is a planning record. MedFinder does not take payment or ship medicine.

Refill calendar

    Reminders

    Reminders stay in this page. Email and phone push are not connected. The same reminder text can be handed to an email or push provider once a patient address or a push subscription exists, by running the reminder check once a day.

    Sellers

    After an order is fulfilled, rate that seller once. Ranking uses a smoothed average, so one 5-star rating does not put a new seller first. Higher scores are listed first and receive more orders when ranking assigns one. The reviews below are demo data.
